US addresses follow: Number Street, City, State ZIP format.
- CTRY_SUB_DVSN is the 2-letter state abbreviation (e.g., NY, CA, TX, DC).
- PST_CD is the 5-digit ZIP or ZIP+4 format (e.g., 10179, 20500-0001).
- TWN_NM is the city name.
- STRT_NM is the street name including directional suffix (e.g., "Pennsylvania Avenue NW").
- BLDG_NB is the street number. Suite/Floor/Apt notation goes to BLDG_NM.
- "PO Box" addresses: box number goes to BLDG_NB, no STRT_NM.
